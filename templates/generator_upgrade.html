Where the numbers come from

Each model's output, current, line breaker, full-load fuel use, inlet and pressure are from the maker's spec sheet. Wire: NEC 310.16 (75 °C), 240.4(B), 250.122 and Chapter 9 conduit fill, voltage drop held to 3% at rated current. Gas: Schedule 40 steel capacity tables (IFGC 402.4 / NFPA 54), natural gas at low pressure with a 0.5 in. w.c. drop, propane at 11 in. w.c. with a 0.5 in. drop. Read the nameplate and the install manual before ordering; the inspector and the gas utility have the final word.
