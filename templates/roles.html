Roles

Two roles: Admins and Employees. Change someone's role under Users in Settings.

Role

Admin

Sees and changes everything.

  • Everything Employees see
  • Settings: users, pay rates, AI agents, the Cooper connection, Jobber, releases
  • Job analytics (revenue, materials, labor, profit)
  • Finance and everyone's timesheets
  • The labor rate, the Labor calculator, and Cooper price updates

Role

Employee

Does the work, without the company's numbers or settings.

  • Quoting, the Pricing Sheet, Services, Clients, Jobs, the Schedule
  • Their own time clock and timesheet, expenses, tasks, training, sales, generators
  • No Settings (users, pay rates, AI agents, Cooper, Jobber, releases)
  • No job analytics, Finance, or anyone else's timesheet or pay
  • No labor rate, Labor calculator, or Cooper price updates
