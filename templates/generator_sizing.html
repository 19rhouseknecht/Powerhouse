How it's calculated

NEC 220.82 optional calculation for a dwelling (the method GenSizer uses): 3 VA per square foot, 1,500 VA for each small-appliance and laundry circuit, every appliance fastened in place at its nameplate; the first 10,000 VA counts in full and the rest at 40%; then the larger of the heating and the cooling. Then NEC 702.4(B): the generator must carry the calculated load, less anything load management keeps off it.
