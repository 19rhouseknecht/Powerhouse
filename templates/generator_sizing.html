How it works

Enter the home, press Size

NEC 220.82: 3 VA per square foot, 1,500 VA for each small-appliance and laundry circuit, every appliance fastened in place at its nameplate; the first 10,000 VA counts in full and the rest at 40%; then the larger of the heating and the cooling.
